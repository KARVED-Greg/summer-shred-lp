The optimal approach

No two strategies are the same

There is no one-size-fits-all. Every approach has merit and shortfalls, so you have to find the one you can stick to. Because consistency is king and that will keep you consistent.

The Bachelor's Approach

For the single guy working long hours. You have fewer external commitments, so meal prep for the week makes more sense. We structure your training around your work schedule and use that extra control to push harder on results.

The Family Man

If you have kids and family dinners are non-negotiable, we front-load your week and your day. We fit in what we need while you have the time, so by the evening you're sitting down with the family without stressing about macros.

The Guy on the Move

If your situation changes week to week, whether that's FIFO, travel for work, or an unpredictable roster, we build strategies that work with your calendar. You stay on track even when your environment doesn't.

There is a common theme in all of this: preparation. To effectively do any approach, you need to plan ahead of time.

We help you figure out which approach fits your life, then build the system around it so you're not guessing every Sunday night.
